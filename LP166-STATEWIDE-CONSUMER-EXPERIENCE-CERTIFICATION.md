# LP166 — Statewide Consumer Experience Certification

## Purpose and boundary
LP166 is the audit-first, final statewide consumer acceptance certification for Gridly's **Know Before You Go** mission. It evaluates whether a first-time Texas consumer can move coherently among search, destinations, routes, awareness, Community Pulse, notifications, Route Watch, reporting, and settings. It does not redesign or alter production UI, search, routing, awareness, notification delivery, runtime membership, deployment, or activation.

## Baseline disclosure
The audited `HEAD` was the expected LP165 baseline `03bbb20fd706966352c68aa05f90a609f725dda5`. The container exposes branch `work`, has no Git remote or `origin/main` ref, and contained pre-existing untracked Android build caches and `node_modules`. Those environmental discrepancies were disclosed and no branch switch, merge, push, deployment, or county activation was performed.

## Consumer entry-point inventory
The repository audit covered application start/onboarding, map and search entry, address/destination/business/category/alias search, destination selection, route generation and preview, route intelligence, Route Watch, Awareness Brief, Community Pulse, Know Before You Go, alert cards, hazard and crossing popups, reporting, notification preferences, and settings. The existing product remains map-and-awareness first; route intelligence is secondary.

## Findings
The deterministic contract contains 24 end-to-end scenarios. Search preserves verified selection and truthful failure. In particular, `274 County Road 677` remains `NO_VERIFIED_RESULT`; no interpolation, nearby-number substitution, road-only promotion, or inferred match is allowed. Route failure cannot become a straight-line route. Quiet awareness does not mean all-clear; active and recently-cleared evidence remain distinct. Consumer evidence answers: what happened, where, how recent, how many reports, and how reliable.

Terminology is consistent around Awareness Brief, Community Pulse, Know Before You Go, Route Watch, Recently cleared, and No verified result. Raw enums, coordinates, internal identifiers, source metadata, and unsupported directional abbreviations remain prohibited consumer output. No proven production defect required a patch.

## Mobile portrait and live boundary
Static repository contracts and prior deterministic evidence confirm that primary search, map, alert, destination, and route actions have portrait-accessible product paths. This run did not execute a physical mobile device, live network route, live awareness source, or operating-system notification delivery. Those observations are not represented as live PASS results.

## Determinism and preservation
Reports use a fixed timestamp, stable identifiers and ordering, sorted JSON keys, and canonical LF. Verification builds twice in isolated temporary directories, compares generated bytes with governed reports, and does not rewrite repository artifacts. Protected identities hash canonical tracked Git-blob bytes, preserving LP164.1 behavior across line-ending conversion.

LP163 truthful routing, LP164 awareness behavior, LP165 notification wording, destination/address/crossing artifacts, and the Liberty benchmark remain unchanged. Runtime is **UNCHANGED**. Deployment and activation are **UNAUTHORIZED**.

## Classification
All 24 deterministic scenarios pass, but earlier live-validation boundaries remain authoritative: LP162 address blockers, LP163 live-network routing, LP164 live awareness, LP165 unimplemented live delivery, and LP166 physical mobile validation. Therefore the evidence supports:
