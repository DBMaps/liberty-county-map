# LP174.3 — Operational Evidence Policy Audit

## Audit boundary and result

LP174.3 is an audit-only review of the ten LP174 `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` facts. It does not ingest a new production evidence artifact, alter LP173/LP174 policy or authorization logic, or change any protected artifact, runtime, or production behavior. The production descriptions supplied for this audit are useful corroborating context, but they do not include the governed metadata record and `sourceArtifactIdentity` required by the existing LP173.1 contract. They therefore cannot be promoted to committed `MACHINE_VERIFIED` evidence without inference.

The audit finds that all ten entries are truthful evidence-ingestion gaps in the current committed evidence set. Three entries (`monitoring.alertDestinations`, `monitoring.alertThresholds`, and `backup.retentionPolicy`) are configurable capabilities, but the supplied evidence establishes neither their configuration nor their authoritative absence. `NOT_CONFIGURED` is already supported by the schema, but using it here would fabricate a positive-absence result. All ten classifications therefore remain `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`; the blocker count remains ten; authorization remains fail-closed and unchanged; and Gridly remains `NOT_READY_FOR_AUTHORIZATION_REASSESSMENT`.

## Root cause

The root cause is not lack of schema vocabulary. LP173 already distinguishes source absence (`SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`) from conclusively verified capability absence (`NOT_CONFIGURED`), and LP173.1 maps only `VERIFIED_ABSENT` metadata with complete provenance to `NOT_CONFIGURED`. The committed production-metadata source contains no production records. The newly described Supabase observations have not been supplied to this repository as secret-safe, metadata-only records with the required source, collection method, verification method, value, evidence type, and canonical SHA-256 source identity. The governance boundary correctly prevents the audit from converting narrative context into machine evidence.

## Policy conclusions

1. **Machine-verifiable facts.** All ten facts require authoritative verification of their current state. Seven primarily record observed metadata; alert destinations, alert thresholds, and retention are configuration capabilities. PITR is also configurable, but the governed fact asks for its current availability state.
2. **Operational-state semantics.** `NOT_CONFIGURED` is an existing supported completion state, not a new evidence category and not a synonym for missing evidence. It is appropriate only when authoritative metadata positively verifies absence. No audited field has that support in the committed evidence.
3. **No truthful blocker removal.** The supplied descriptions identify a viable evidence source for several fields, but source availability in the world is distinct from validated evidence availability to the deterministic collector. An audit-only milestone cannot manufacture the missing provenance record.
4. **Updated blocker count.** `OWNER_ACTION_REQUIRED` remains 0; `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` remains 10; total blockers remain 10.
5. **Authorization.** Deployment, activation, distribution, production restore, production rollback, and public launch remain `NOT_AUTHORIZED`. No operation is performed and authorization logic is unchanged.
6. **Readiness.** Gridly remains `NOT_READY_FOR_AUTHORIZATION_REASSESSMENT`. Once sanitized authoritative records with complete provenance and source identity are ingested through the existing workflow, a separate deterministic reassessment may evaluate readiness; that future possibility is not a present authorization.
