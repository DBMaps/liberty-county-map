# LP177.1 — Eleven-county prerequisite audit

## Scope and decision boundary

This is an audit of the governed repository baseline at `1b8e185`. It changes no
runtime, certification, authorization, production operation, or governance. In
particular, it does not recertify a package or revise an authorization decision.
It tests only whether the county evidence cited by LP176/LP177 supports treating
all eleven counties as a present activation prerequisite.

## Determination

**The eleven-county prerequisite is not supported as a blanket activation
blocker by the current governed county evidence. Completely retire that grouped
prerequisite.** The evidence does support eleven narrower, county-specific
certification restrictions: none of these counties may be represented as
address-certified, and an attempt to activate any one of them *on that premise*
must stop at LP132 Gate 2. Those restrictions are not evidence that activation
of a different governed county, or activation as a platform operation, depends
on certifying all eleven.

The updated activation-blocker count attributable to the grouped LP176/LP177
prerequisite is **0** (previously one grouped prerequisite covering eleven
counties). The count of county-specific certification restrictions remains
**11**. Other LP177 prerequisites are outside this audit and are neither retired
nor reclassified here.

## Root cause

LP135 records `243` certified counties and `11` counties whose immutable LP130
package bytes are unavailable in the checkout. It expressly limits the impact
of every affected county to: “LP132 Gate 2 remains closed; this does not change
activation or runtime status.” LP132 makes certification one gate in a
**county's** dossier and says certification alone neither satisfies Gates 3–7
nor authorizes activation.

LP167 correctly described the result as eleven **county-specific restrictions**,
but also carried them into its general activation result. LP176 preserved that
policy without a new county evaluation. LP177 then collapsed the restrictions
into one “Eleven-county address certification” prerequisite and cited LP135,
even though LP135 does not classify the eleven as a blanket activation blocker.
Thus package unavailability was transformed from a county-scoped certification
evidence gap into a universal activation dependency without a current governed
report establishing that dependency. That transformed dependency is a
**Legacy prerequisite**.

## Governed evidence and interpretation

Only machine-readable governed artifacts and the operative gate definition were
used for county status; historical prose summaries were not used as proof.

* `evidence/lp135/statewide-certification.json` is the current statewide county
  disposition. It identifies the eleven counties, their FIPS values, immutable
  identities, `CERTIFICATION_BLOCKED` status, `PACKAGE_AVAILABILITY` stage,
  `LOCAL_PACKAGE_UNAVAILABLE` classification, corrective action, and limited
  readiness impact.
* `evidence/lp134/certification-results.json#/remaining/0` through
  `#/remaining/10` independently enumerate the current unresolved inputs. Every
  entry says the immutable package is not present in the checkout and that the
  status is retained pending byte-identical recertification. These are missing
  inputs, not observed certification failures.
* `evidence/lp130/final-reconciliation.json` supplies the governed package and
  sidecar identities referenced by LP135. It supports **manufactured/recorded,
  payload unavailable in this checkout**, not “package never existed.”
* `LP132-TEXAS-STATEWIDE-ACTIVATION-ROADMAP.md` defines Gate 2 as a county-level
  certification gate and requires a county with a Gate 2 failure to remain
  inactive. It also states that certification alone does not authorize
  activation.

There is no current observed certifier FAIL for any of the eleven and no current
report identifies a defect in certification logic. The truthful package status
for every row is therefore **manufactured and identity-recorded by LP130; exact
payload unavailable in this checkout**. The truthful certification status is
**blocked before certification at package availability**, not certification
failed.

## Recommendation

**Completely retire prerequisite** `LP167-B001` as a blanket prerequisite in a
future authorized governance action. Preserve, without changing them in this
audit, the eleven county-specific `CERTIFICATION_BLOCKED` dispositions and LP132
Gate 2 behavior. A future authorization reassessment should count only blockers
whose current evidence establishes applicability to the operation being
considered; it should not require all eleven certifications unless a new
governed artifact expressly makes statewide, simultaneous activation the scope.

All eleven counties lack current evidence supporting continued blockage of
activation **outside their own county dossiers**. None is certified by this
finding, and none is authorized for activation by this audit.
