# LP188.6 — Owner Promotion Eligibility Review and Authorization

## Decision

All 254 LP188.5 FIPS/SHA-256 package identities satisfy the structural prerequisites for metadata-only promotion. No repository artifact explicitly authorizes promotion of these LP188.3 identities. LP188.6 therefore stops at `PROMOTION_ELIGIBILITY_CERTIFIED_OWNER_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`: zero identities are owner-authorized, zero are promoted, and zero counties are newly activated.

## Authorization audit

LP140 is explicitly non-authorizing and keeps Gate 7 separate. LP143 defines a schema-valid, county-FIPS-specific activation record plus affirmative operational, deployment, runtime, geometry, and membership approvals as activation authority; its registry contains zero authorizations. LP144 remains governance-only and non-authorizing. LP152 and LP153 record zero deployment/activation authorizations and executions. Later LP167 decisions are capability-specific `NOT_AUTHORIZED` launch, deployment, and activation decisions. None mentions or binds metadata-only promotion to an LP188.5 SHA-256, so none can be reused.

Repository governance consequently supports explicit county-FIPS decisions and governed cohorts/batches, but separates authority by capability. LP188.6 offers four exact-identity selections for owner choice: all 254 identities, all 243 unrestricted identities, an explicit county-FIPS list, or an explicit governed batch. Every selected entry must retain its LP188.5 FIPS, SHA-256, package schema, metadata-only promotion scope, and an explicit `OWNER_AUTHORIZED` status. Display names and paths are not authority.

## Structural prerequisites and authority boundary

Structural eligibility requires the authoritative LP188.5 certification, 254 unique valid FIPS identities, exact stable SHA-256 values, supported package schema, no missing/duplicate/mismatched identity, no validation failure, and proven runtime isolation. Identity capture does not imply structural eligibility; structural eligibility does not imply owner authorization; authorization does not imply promotion; and promotion does not imply activation eligibility or activation.

The 11 restrictions remain preserved with their original reasons and activation blocked. Restricted identities may be selected for metadata-only promotion, but that selection cannot clear a restriction. The 28 operational counties retain their existing runtime status and also require separate authorization for promotion of the new LP188.3 identity. LP188.6 writes reports only and neither reads package bytes nor changes runtime consumption.

## Owner decision required

The owner must choose one of the supported scopes (or decline), review the exact FIPS/SHA-256 bindings in `reports/lp1886/owner-promotion-authorization-request.json`, and explicitly record authorization in a later governed decision artifact. This request is a non-authorizing template. Until that decision exists, all 254 promotion records remain blocked and `promotedCount` remains zero.
