## Contract and boundary

The tool requires the exact eleven FIPS, restored or already-exact governed payload identity, equal expected/actual byte length and SHA-256, two referenced LP134 `PASS` certificates bound to that same identity, deterministic agreement, and `restrictionCanBeCleared: true`. Missing or inconsistent evidence fails closed per county.

Only the LP190.3 JSON and Markdown evidence outputs are written. Production application, package registries, geometry, consumer availability, and Crossing Packages remain untouched. Runtime therefore remains 243 operational and 11 restricted pending a separately governed activation milestone.
