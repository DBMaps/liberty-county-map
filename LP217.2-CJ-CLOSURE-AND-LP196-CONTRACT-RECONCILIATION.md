# LP217.2 — C/J Closure and LP196 Contract Reconciliation

## Scope and decision

LP217.2 reconciles tests and records closure evidence only. It makes **no production runtime, static membership, presentation-coordinate, crossing, roadway, or geometry change**. The authoritative operational identity is now:

> canonical Census PLACE + explicit governed county membership

A multi-county PLACE remains canonical for presentation, but activation, persistence, rehydration, selection, and downstream county-scoped consumers must retain a valid county authority. Missing or invalid authority fails closed; membership order, the first label, and the previously active county are not authority.

## Repair history

### LP216 root cause

LP216 instrumentation isolated the C/J defect as a loss of county-qualified authority while a canonical multi-county PLACE moved through selection and state transitions. County-neutral/label-only reconstruction could substitute the first matching membership and allow downstream consumers to converge on the wrong county.

### LP217 repair

LP217 made governed county membership explicit, validated it against the PLACE membership set, persisted it, projected the canonical PLACE into that county, synchronized active county and county-governed consumers, and failed closed when authority was absent or invalid.

### LP217.1 repair

LP217.1 preserved membership-specific result identity in the picker. Rows sharing a PLACE GEOID remain distinct by county, and selection/current-state comparison includes the county membership rather than reducing identity to the shared label or PLACE token.
