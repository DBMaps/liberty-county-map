# LP241.10 — Statewide local business / POI search certification

**Evidence date:** 2026-08-28
**Executive result:** **FAIL — PROVIDER_CAPABILITY_INSUFFICIENT / HYBRID_PROVIDER_REQUIRED.** This audit does not confuse address resolution with POI discovery and does not claim statewide readiness without live evidence.

## 1. Executive result

Gridly has a functional POI-shaped search pipeline, but the repository does not prove practically useful named-business and category discovery across Texas. LP097 supplies 18 Liberty records, including two Walmart stores; LP098 expands civic/public-place templates to 28 counties, not 254. The remote boundary forwards free-form search to Nominatim, but neither checked-in evidence nor deterministic live captures establish statewide/rural POI completeness. Launch certification therefore fails honestly.

A bounded repair is included: contextual business/category requests use the governed county viewbox where available, results outside 75 miles are suppressed unless they agree with the active locality or bounds, and a read-only acceptance helper reports the actual provider candidates and context.

## 2. Current POI search architecture

The runtime is `runGridlyLiveDestinationSearch` → `gridlySearchAddress`. Candidate sources are saved places, LP097/LP098 curated records, and the `gridly-geocode` Edge Function. Results are normalized, LP099/LP101-classified, scored with name/category/geography/distance signals, containment/relevance filtered, deduplicated, rendered, and handed to the common destination selection path.

* **LP097:** governed curated destinations, mostly Liberty; supplies names, categories, address text, coordinates, community/county, and source metadata.
* **LP098:** preserves LP097 and adds five templated public-place categories for the existing 28-county cohort. It is not a chain/business inventory.
* **LP099:** intent aliases and result scoring only. Its historical 112-case “certification” is synthetic classification/ranking—not provider coverage evidence.
* **LP101:** normalization, category recognition, relevance, and scoring only; it does not supply POIs.
* **`gridly-geocoding-client`:** browser-to-Supabase boundary and canonical response validation.
* **`gridly-geocode`:** calls configured Nominatim for free-form queries with `countrycodes=us`, optional viewbox, and address details. Certified TxGIO and rural adapters apply to address intent, not POI inventory.
* **LP102 / LP104:** rural/address certification and exact address packages. They do not establish named-business or category discovery.

No current source supplies verified phone or website fields. Provider relevance is implicit in Nominatim order; Gridly adds deterministic local scoring.

## 5. Named business result

**NOT CERTIFIED STATEWIDE.** Local curated evidence exists for Walmart only in Liberty and Cleveland. Nominatim may return named chains, but repository evidence does not prove Walmart, H-E-B, CVS, Walgreens, Starbucks, or McDonald's coverage/ranking across the cohort. The repair ensures an actually returned nearby candidate is preferred and distant candidates cannot masquerade as local.

## 6. Category search result

**PARTIAL / NOT CERTIFIED.** LP101 recognizes a subset of categories, while LP241.10 explicitly distinguishes `CATEGORY_DISCOVERY` for gas station, hospital, pharmacy, restaurant, grocery store, hotel, school, and urgent care from `NAMED_ENTITY_SEARCH`. The upstream may answer these, but statewide category completeness is unknown.

## 7. Locality / proximity authority

The observed runtime precedence is:

1. selected Home/Awareness Area and its governed coordinate/county;
2. current map center when no selected awareness area exists;
3. Liberty default center as bounded legacy fallback.

An explicit location embedded in a query is handled by the existing out-of-area logic. There is no separate live GPS authority passed into this search path. LP241.10 preserves Home Area governance. The helper snapshots context before and after searching and flags any change as stale-context detection.

## 8. Ranking quality result

Ranking combines provider order, LP099 exact/alias strength, LP101 term/category/geography match, confidence, saved/curated authority, locality/county/bounds agreement, title match, and haversine distance. LP241.10 adds a clear publication boundary: contextual POI results over **75 miles** are not local unless within governed bounds or locality-agreeing. Distances are reported only when coordinates permit haversine computation.

## 9. No-result policy

A contextual POI response with no candidate inside 75 miles (and no bounds/locality agreement) is a truthful no-local-result. The helper returns `localResultAvailable: false` and `pass: false`. It never mutates or reuses a selected destination. The UI retains its existing no-matching-destination state; there is no silent statewide/global promotion.

## 10. Destination handoff result

Existing normalized results retain name, structured/raw address, coordinates, locality metadata, provider identity, and rank. They continue through the common renderer/selection flow; no business-only route bypass was added. The helper reports handoff availability from finite coordinates without selecting anything. Full live Route Watch handoff across the statewide cohort remains unexecuted and therefore uncertified.

## 11. Data sufficiency classification

**HYBRID_PROVIDER_REQUIRED**, with current state **PROVIDER_CAPABILITY_INSUFFICIENT** and curated state **LOCAL_DATASET_INSUFFICIENT**. The smallest viable architecture is the existing server boundary plus a provider/source with contractually suitable named POI and category/nearby search, normalized and locally reranked behind Gridly. No paid provider is selected by this audit.

## 12. Exact defects found

1. Prior LP099 counts proved local JavaScript classification, not statewide provider coverage.
2. Business intent did not request the available governed viewbox; only generic-local intent did.
3. Far-away suppression applied only to generic-local search and could treat any Texas result as acceptable.
4. Curated content covers 28 counties and is overwhelmingly templated public facilities, not statewide practical businesses.
5. No governed statewide live cohort captures provider result name, coordinates, distance, locality, county, or closer-candidate ordering.
6. Repository evidence cannot establish Nominatim rural completeness, production capacity, or use/storage terms.
