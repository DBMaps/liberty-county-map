# LP241.15C — POI runtime schema v2 reconciliation

LP241.15C establishes the non-production `gridly.poi.runtime.v2` successor contract without changing the frozen authority. The authority remains `lp24111-d5-standalone-2026-08-28` with 391,772 governed POIs.

V1 remains immutable historical evidence. Its required POI-level `communityIdentity` made its cache entries incompatible with v2; neither shard nor result caches may cross schema namespaces.

V2 assigns `communityIdentity` only to a governed request context originating from a canonical PLACE or governed non-PLACE. Map-center, county-only, rural-coordinate, direct-coordinate, and unincorporated requests are valid without it. A returned POI never inherits the request identity and always retains its own mandatory `countyContextId`, including cross-boundary results.

Future county projection is deterministic: frozen `county_fips` is matched by exact equality against the 254-entry governed Texas county registry and must resolve exactly once. Spatial, nearest-county, and display-text inference are forbidden.

The existing LP241.13 legal package and exact NOTICE are rebound rather than rewritten. License exposure, modification wording, non-affiliation wording, and the `DATA_SOURCES_AND_LICENSES` attribution target are unchanged. Active-search attribution persistence is not certified because the provider remains inactive.

No shards were materialized, no Parquet reader was added, no provider or production gate was activated, and no deployment or Supabase mutation occurred. The next bounded step may evaluate a pinned Node-native Parquet reader for shard materialization against this stable contract. LP241.14 rehearsal remains ineligible until shards exist.
