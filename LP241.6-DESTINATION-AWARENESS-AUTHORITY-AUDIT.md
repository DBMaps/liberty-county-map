# LP241.6 Destination Search vs Awareness Authority Audit

## Decision

`selectGridlySearchResult()` is a destination command, not a governed Home Area command. It normalizes the chosen row, writes `activeResult` and `selectedDestination`, updates destination presentation, creates the destination marker, and starts the route preview. It does not call the Home Area persistence or awareness synchronization paths. Box Canyon therefore should remain the selected awareness area after choosing Chambers County Courthouse.

The governed Home Area changes only through the explicit ZIP/manual confirmation flow ending in `gridlyApplyConfirmedHomePersonalization()`. That transaction persists the canonical home record, updates compatibility settings, changes active county/community/awareness state, synchronizes awareness surfaces, and focuses the confirmed home selection.

## Chambers destination authority

The governed local seed identifies Chambers County Courthouse at `29.7730, -94.6827`, in Anahuac, Chambers County (`chambers-tx`, FIPS `48071`). At selection time, however, the authoritative destination is the normalized selected result (including its provider identity and coordinates), and the route preview copies that identity and coordinate. The live `gridlyDestinationAuthorityAudit()` helper reports the selected result rather than assuming the seed won provider ranking.

## Destination Intelligence authority

The current surface is route-corridor intelligence, despite its Destination Intelligence heading. After OSRM returns geometry, the runtime evaluates a 750-foot corridor along the complete origin-to-destination route. It spatially matches:

- active hazards;
- the Alerts surface snapshot;
- active community reports; and
- the statewide crossing collection.

It does not directly query an Official Roadways collection. Weather can only corroborate an already matched route record and, today, its locality check is tied to the selected awareness area. The visible `Quiet conditions` decision means the route impact audit found no hazard, alert, or community-report signal that raised the route impact above `none`; crossings alone do not raise that decision.

This is not proof of Chambers endpoint-only intelligence. It is proof of origin-to-Chambers route-corridor intelligence. Selected-awareness collections can be inputs, but their records cannot become matches merely because Box Canyon is selected: coordinate-bearing records must fall within 750 feet of the route. Live-proximity Route Watch incidents are also admitted by their own route relevance path. The weather corroboration rule cannot manufacture a quiet decision and cannot turn unrelated Box Canyon weather into a Chambers condition; it only decorates a qualifying matched record.

No positive Box Canyon record leak is proven by the reported quiet state. A more important authority gap **is** proven by source inspection: hazards, alerts, and community reports are read from the already loaded/current consumer collections; destination selection does not load Chambers-scoped Weather, Official Roadways, Alerts, or Community evidence before saying `Quiet conditions`. The 420-mile geometry filter prevents an off-route Box Canyon record from becoming a match, but it cannot find Chambers records that were never loaded. Crossings are the only explicitly statewide collection in this calculation, and crossings alone do not make the decision non-quiet. Therefore the observed copy is “no impact found in available route inputs,” not certified Chambers intelligence. This is a destination-authority/coverage defect if the heading promises conditions at or along the selected destination journey.

The smallest proposed repair is not an awareness mutation. It is to give the destination route-intelligence builder an explicitly destination/route-scoped evidence snapshot (with county coverage metadata), and to suppress `Quiet conditions` in favor of an unavailable/incomplete-coverage state until the destination county and route corridor have authoritative source coverage. The bounded authority-signature repair is documented below.

Chambers remains **not passed** until the owner snapshot confirms the selected normalized coordinate/county, route endpoints, match identities/counts, source coverage, and final decision in the same session.

## LP241.6 snapshot RCA and repair

`gridlyDestinationAuthorityAudit()` was not returning one stored monolithic
Destination Intelligence object. It obtained cached corridor intelligence and
impact objects, then constructed coverage and the final decision. The stale owner
was the authority boundary in `getGridlyDestinationCoverageState()`: unlike the
outer audit, it only read `selectedDestination.countyId`/`raw.countyId`. A
normalized provider result can have valid Chambers coordinates before it has an
explicit county field. The outer audit resolved those coordinates and therefore
published `selectedDestination.countyId: chambers-tx`, while coverage invoked the
reducer without that resolved destination county. The adjacent projections were
describing different authority inputs.

`existingDestinationIntelligencePreserved: true` is a presentation-contract
constant from LP063, not the cache decision that preserved a Liberty coverage
snapshot. The corridor caches were keyed by route geometry, Route Watch, live
position, and source records, but omitted destination/coverage authority. Opening
the pane merely read those caches; it did not invalidate them. Route-ready primed
them, while destination selection and route-preview clearing explicitly
invalidated them. Destination county resolution alone could not change their key.

The repair resolves coordinate-based destination county authority before coverage
construction. It gives the coverage snapshot a semantic signature covering the
awareness county, destination identity/county, route-origin authority, route
destination, and required scope. A matching signature preserves the snapshot; a
mismatch records invalidation and builds the next generation. The signature also
participates in corridor intelligence/impact cache identity.

The direct reducer control with `liberty-tx` awareness and `chambers-tx`
destination produces route counties `[liberty-tx, chambers-tx]`. The five
destination/route families are missing even if current-awareness collections are
available, because Liberty authority cannot certify Chambers; statewide crossings
alone is completed. Coverage is `COVERAGE_PARTIAL`, with an incomplete decision
and “Route conditions are still being checked.” Valid matched hazards remain in
the independent impact calculation and remain visible under partial coverage.
