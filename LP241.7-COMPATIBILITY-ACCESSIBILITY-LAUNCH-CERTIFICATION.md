# LP241.7 Owner Device Availability Reconciliation

**As of:** 2026-08-27  
**Final classification:** **OWNER_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED**
**Evidence boundary:** no physical Android execution is claimed. `DETERMINISTIC_EMULATION_CONTRACT` means bounded mobile-condition source-contract evidence; no rendered browser emulation was executed, not an Android-device pass.

## 1. Android device requirement RCA

OA-2 became mandatory because source inspection could not prove physical touch, mobile Chrome UI occlusion, platform font rendering, or rotation repaint. That conservative boundary was incorrectly converted into a requirement that the owner personally possess Android hardware. Gridly's existing launch policy does not require physical pre-launch certification on every supported mobile platform. The appropriate contract is deterministic cross-platform compatibility, physical acceptance on available representative platforms, no known defects, and post-launch monitoring of additional combinations. With no known Android defect, unavailable hardware is not itself a launch risk or blocker.

## 3. What can be certified without Android hardware

The deterministic audit certifies zoom permission, safe-area tokens, dynamic viewport units, responsive breakpoints, reduced motion, scrollable overlays, manifest standalone mode, icons, and service-worker lifecycle wiring. The bounded Android-like source-contract scenarios are labeled **DETERMINISTIC_EMULATION_CONTRACT**: 390×844 portrait, 844×390 landscape, and 390×844 at 200% text/zoom pressure, with touch and coarse-pointer conditions. They materially cover layout selection, reflow, safe/dynamic viewport sizing, sheet scrolling, and the control reachability contract. They do not assert Android font rasterization, physical Chrome occlusion, gesture-region behavior, or compositor repaint.

## 4. Whether Android physical testing is a launch blocker

**No.** None of the remaining Android-only observations is a distinct required product claim in the current Gridly launch policy, and no known defect points to a heightened Android risk. OA-2 is therefore `DETERMINISTIC_COMPATIBILITY_PASS`; physical Android is `OPTIONAL_POST_LAUNCH_VALIDATION`, never `PHYSICAL_DEVICE_PASS`.

## 5. PWA platform requirement RCA

OA-4's journey tests generic product behavior: installability, standalone launch/scope, cold launch, reachable controls, My Area persistence, close/relaunch lifecycle, a nonblank shell, and service-worker-controlled startup. It was coupled to Android only because OA-2 previously supplied a convenient same device. Android is not intrinsic to those product claims.

## 6. OA-4 width-gate reconciliation

**Yes, with an explicit supported-viewport boundary.** The authoritative runtime selects the supported portrait owner only when the CSS viewport is **980 px wide or narrower and portrait** (height greater than or equal to width). At 981 px or wider the portrait cleanup owner is ineligible. On a Windows fine-pointer device, a landscape window remains desktop (or, at sufficiently short dimensions, the separately gated tactical-landscape mode), even when its width is 980 px or narrower. The practical owner target is therefore a tall window such as an approximately 800×900 CSS-pixel content viewport; the visible transition from the desktop board to the mobile shell confirms the runtime has entered the supported surface.

The installed Edge app is resizable and the runtime re-evaluates layout ownership on window and visual-viewport resize. The owner evidence already establishes successful installation, standalone-window launch, and a nonblank shell at the intentional desktop-development gate. That evidence is a **generic PWA install/standalone lifecycle pass**, not an in-app mobile presentation pass and not full desktop PWA support. The remaining OA-4 action is to resize that same installed standalone window into the supported portrait viewport and exercise Alerts, My Area, close/relaunch, and persistence there.

OA-4 is therefore redefined exactly as **“Installed Windows Edge PWA, supported mobile-width viewport.”** “Background/foreground” remains translated to minimize/restore or close/relaunch; close/relaunch is the required persistence gate because desktop process suspension is OS-managed and nondeterministic.

## 7. Any remaining Android-only PWA claim

Windows cannot cover Android WebAPK packaging, Android task/background eviction, Android permission prompts, or Chrome/Android OS update integration. These are not claims made by the generic Gridly PWA launch gate and remain optional post-launch observations. If Gridly later promises Android-specific packaging or lifecycle behavior, that promise requires a separately owned physical-Android gate.
