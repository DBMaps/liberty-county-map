## 7. Service-worker/cache result

Current checked-in behavior installs immediately (`skipWaiting`), claims clients on activation, deletes other `gridly-pwa-shell-*` and `gridly-beta-closure-*` caches, and retains `gridly-pwa-shell-lp235-v1`. Navigations are network-first with `cache: "no-store"`, updating cached `index.html` on success and falling back to it only on network failure. `js/app.js` is **not** in the explicit shell list and is not intercepted by the service worker. Explicit shell assets and the two county geometry assets use cache-first behavior; the latter can remain cached until the worker/cache advances.

Therefore:

- **A, old deployed artifact:** proven for the last recorded deployment.
- **B, current deployment controlled by an old worker/cache:** possible in an already-used browser but not proven. Navigation normally prefers network; an old controller can still return its old cached `index.html` when network fetch fails, and cache-first governed assets may remain old.
- **C, both:** possible, not proven.
- **D, neither:** not supportable from available evidence.
