## Root cause and implemented journey

The authority was already shared, but presentation accumulated milestone-specific entry gates. ZIP, county, direct search, and manual search appeared as different traveler decisions even though they converged on governed records.

LP241.9 makes the direct community/ZIP search the visible primary surface in onboarding and Settings:

**Set or Change Home Area → Search your Texas community → select an explicitly county-qualified governed result → Use this Home Area → validate → persist and activate.**

ZIP remains an input form handled by `resolveGridlyAwarenessAreaQuery`, not a separate screen. “Use my current location” follows search as a secondary convenience. The legacy county and community controls and ZIP prototype utilities remain in code for compatibility, but are hidden from the traveler path. The standalone manual gate is removed.

No registry, eligibility rule, resolver, persistence schema, Cloudflare configuration, provider authority, routing, map, report, destination search, or launch gate is changed.

## LP241.9A owner-acceptance RCA and repair

The deployed Portrait V2 Settings surface was owned by `buildSettingsSurfaceHtml()`, not by the already-simplified legacy Settings markup. That runtime builder retained the LP051.7 Home ZIP card and a second Current view card. Its `settings-change-home-zip` action called `gridlyOpenLp0516ZipConfirmationPrototype()` (the ZIP-only modal), while `settings-change-awareness-area` and `settings-choose-available-areas` separately called `openGridlySettingsAvailableAreaPicker()`. The shared governed search therefore existed, but remained secondary behind the old action hierarchy; the visible Portrait markup and its action bridge outranked the LP241.9 legacy markup.

LP241.9A replaces those Portrait cards with one Home Area card and one `settings-change-home-area` action. That action calls `openGridlyPrimaryHomeAreaChooser()` directly, which renders the shared governed community/ZIP picker in consumer mode. Onboarding continues to render the same search-first concept directly. The standalone ZIP prototype remains isolated for diagnostic compatibility and no longer has a traveler-facing Settings or onboarding binding.

The acceptance helper now reports and gates on `changeAreaOpensPrimarySearchDirectly`, `zipOnlyModalRequired`, `chooseManuallyRequired`, `chooseAvailableAreasPrimary`, `separateHomeZipPrimary`, and `primaryJourneyStepCount`. This makes the owner-proven ZIP-first journey fail acceptance even when a secondary community chooser exists elsewhere in the DOM.
