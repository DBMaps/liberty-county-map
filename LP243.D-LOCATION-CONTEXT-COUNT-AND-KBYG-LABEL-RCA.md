## KBYG RCA

`gridlyTravelBriefRoadwayConditionLabel` was a KBYG-only semantic formatter with hard-coded mixed casing (`Construction`, but `road closure`, `lane closure`, `crash`, and `flooding`). Consolidation joined those values as condition labels, producing the internally inconsistent phrase. The formatter now derives a canonical condition key and delegates the label role to `gridlyConditionDisplayLabel`; an unknown condition safely becomes `Roadway Condition`. The Port Arthur literal is not patched and no taxonomy is added.
