# LP094.1 — Launch Surface Clarity Polish

## Executive summary

LP094.1 applies a deliberately small presentation polish to Gridly’s launch surfaces. Consumer-facing county choices now use county names without internal qualification, the portrait dock presents persistent labels beneath its existing icons, and Settings copy no longer describes testing, cleanup, development, or beta status. No product logic or protected runtime was changed.

## Changes made

- Changed the onboarding San Jacinto option from “San Jacinto County (validation only)” to “San Jacinto County.” Its value, county metadata, activation behavior, inventory, ZIP resolution, and manual-selection path remain intact.
- Made the existing Report, Alerts, History, and Settings dock names visually persistent. Icon and destination order, handlers, and tap targets remain unchanged.
- Removed development-delivery notices, the cleanup/test-tools explanation, and beta-status presentation from Settings and About.
- Replaced the V2 Settings availability language with concise consumer copy while retaining preference controls, Appearance, installation, privacy, safety, feedback, version, and About content.
- Added `window.gridlyLp0941LaunchSurfaceAudit?.()` as a read-only presentation audit.

## Before and after

Before LP094.1, a county option exposed the internal phrase “validation only,” dock names were available to assistive technology but hidden visually, and Settings explained development state and absent internal tools. After LP094.1, counties are presented consistently by name, each primary dock icon has a short visible label, and Settings reads as finished consumer product copy.

## Browser certification

Automated source and audit checks pass, but genuine browser certification remains pending because this environment contains no browser executable and its package policy rejects Playwright installation with HTTP 403. Setup, navigation, all dock destinations, Settings, Report, Alerts, Community Pulse, Travel Brief, and the Historical Intelligence boundary therefore require a final device/browser pass before merge.

## Protected systems confirmation

LP094.1 changes only HTML copy/label semantics, Settings presentation copy, the passive audit, and this record. Awareness filtering, reporting behavior, Route Watch, Destination Intelligence, Supabase, service-worker behavior, PWA runtime, county activation, ZIP mapping, and other protected systems were not modified.

## Historical Intelligence confirmation
