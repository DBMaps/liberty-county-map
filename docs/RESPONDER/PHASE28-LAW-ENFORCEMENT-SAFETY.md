# Law-enforcement operational-awareness safety contract

**GRIDLY DISPATCH PILOT IS NOT A SYSTEM OF RECORD FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT.**

It is not CAD, RMS, evidence management, or a 911 platform. It is not approved or authorized for CJIS-regulated data. No CJIS compliance, certification, or law-enforcement-sensitive-data support is claimed. Only a future separately certified architecture and explicit authorization could change that posture.

Police pilot use is limited to controlled operational awareness: synthetic roadway incident awareness, temporary traffic impact, scene-related travel restriction, a suitably sanitized public-safety coordination notice, Public Works coordination, barricade/traffic-control awareness, and event travel impact. A private road-restriction record does not authorize public road-closure publication.

Do not store investigative details; suspect, victim, witness or juvenile information; CJIS content; criminal history; evidence; body-camera media; officer tactical plans; confidential informants; NCIC/TCIC data; police reports; dispatch/911 call details; or protected personal data. This applies to titles, summaries, private JSON, comments, attachments, exports, test fixtures, audit reasons and support logs. No such operational examples are seeded.

Public Works scenarios include a water leak affecting travel, planned road work, drainage/flooding observation, debris/obstruction, traffic-control work, infrastructure condition, public operational notice, planned utility work, and municipal maintenance affecting travel. Each begins as a private CONDITION, HAZARD, PLANNED_WORK or OPERATIONAL_NOTICE. Hazard/road-closure content cannot be relabeled as a condition or notice to evade publishing gates.

The command requires an OPERATIONAL_AWARENESS_ONLY attestation for record creation/update. Internal and consumer shares require separate sanitation attestations and consumer review is independent from its author. These are workflow controls, not automated guarantees that text is free of prohibited content. Production Police readiness requires training, approved field limits, review, incident containment and a validated prohibited-content response process. Do not onboard Police by weakening this boundary.

If prohibited data is discovered: contain access and sharing, withdraw/invalidate affected projections, preserve only required incident evidence under controlled access, and route to the authorized privacy/security process. Do not copy the material into a bug report, new fixture or consumer projection. Legal/privacy disposition must be approved separately.
