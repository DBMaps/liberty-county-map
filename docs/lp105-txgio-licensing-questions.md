# TxGIO/TNRIS written-authorization questions

This is a governance request, not legal advice. Please obtain written answers from TxGIO/TNRIS or the applicable data owner and retain the evidence reference before changing the fail-closed decision record.

1. May Gridly publicly redistribute county-derived address packages from the statewide dataset?
2. May Gridly create and publish normalized derivative packages?
3. May a public browser download and use those packages client-side?
4. Is distribution of residential address points expressly permitted?
5. What exact attribution wording is required?
6. What update, correction, takedown, or removal obligations apply?
7. Are there restrictions on commercial use?
8. Are caching or offline retention restricted?
9. Are transformed coordinates or transformed address attributes restricted from republication?
10. Does the authorization apply to all Texas counties statewide, including contributing-source records?

Public availability, government ownership, prior Liberty processing, silence in visible terms, and completion of LP105 are not authorization.
