Gridly Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

This policy explains the information Gridly processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you. Gridly is operated by DJ Burns Collective LLC, doing business as Gridly App, at gridlygo.com. Initial service and condition coverage is focused on Texas in the United States. Gridly does not require an account and does not sell personal information. Gridly has no advertising SDK.

Information you choose to provide

Community reports can include a condition type, severity, details, time, and map location. Feedback can include text you submit. Do not include names, license plates, contact details, or other personal information in community content.

Location and map activity

With permission, Gridly uses foreground approximate or precise location to center the map, find nearby conditions, support Route Watch, and place a report you choose to submit. Gridly does not request background location. Searches, selected places, route endpoints, map coordinates, and addresses may be sent to mapping, geocoding, weather, roadway, or service providers needed to answer the request.

Device and operational data

Gridly creates a random local device identifier for report ownership, duplicate prevention, abuse controls, and deletion verification. It is not publicly displayed. The service stores the raw device-to-report association in a private table only during the report’s bounded live-retention period. Moderation complaints and deletion requests use one-way digests, operation digests, status, timestamps, and bounded reviewer outcomes. We do not use these records for advertising or cross-service tracking.

Local-only data

Settings, saved places, walkthrough state, accepted community terms version, Route Watch state, and reports you hide are generally stored on this device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may erase them. A local Hide does not delete shared content.

How information is used

  • Provide maps, local awareness, search, weather, routes, and community reporting.
  • Prevent replay, spam, unsafe content, fraud, and misuse.
  • Review complaints, quarantine or remove content, suppress abusive sources, and process verified deletion requests.
  • Diagnose reliability and comply with law.

Sharing

Public community reports show only the report content and public report fields, not device identifiers, complaint identities, deletion records, or moderator notes. Gridly shares limited data with infrastructure and data providers as needed to operate features, and may disclose information when legally required or needed to protect safety and rights. Gridly does not sell or rent personal information.

Retention

Live community reports and their private device links are scheduled for deletion by day 149 and never beyond 180 days. Normal report deletion cascades the private link. Replay protection keeps only a one-way operation digest without a report or device reference. Complaint and deletion linkages are cleared by day 149; completed deletion requests are shortened to a 90-day window. Source suppressions expire no later than the source report’s linkage deadline and never beyond 180 days. Aggregated monthly counts contain no report, location, or device identifier.

Your choices and requests

You may deny location permission and use manual map selection where available. You may hide a community report locally. Use Delete mine on a report submitted from this device to request verified deletion while its private link exists. You may also open Privacy in Settings and use Feedback for access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions. Some requests may require enough information to locate and verify the data without exposing another person’s report.

Security, age, and changes

Gridly uses access controls, private schemas, row-level security, bounded fields, and least-privilege database functions, but no system is perfectly secure. Gridly is intended only for users age 18 and older and is not directed to minors. People under 18 must not use Gridly or submit personal information through it. Gridly may restrict access for users Google has determined to be minors. We may update this policy and will revise its effective date when material changes are made.

Contact

Open Gridly Settings → Support → Privacy & deletion or Send Feedback, or email privacy@gridlygo.com. Applicable legal rights and regulator complaint routes are not limited by this policy.
