Gridly Community Guidelines

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Acceptance version: gridly-ugc-2026-09-17-v2

Community reports are shared observations, not official traffic information or emergency reports. If anyone may be in danger, call 911 or the appropriate authority. Never use Gridly while driving.

Share useful, first-hand observations

Post only road, rail-crossing, weather, or travel-condition information you personally observed and reasonably believe is current and accurate. Choose the closest category, use the mapped location carefully, and update or clear a report when conditions change.

Do not post

  • False, deceptive, manipulated, or deliberately misleading information.
  • Threats, harassment, bullying, hate, discrimination, sexual content, or glorification of violence.
  • Names, contact details, license plates, faces, precise home locations, or other personal or confidential information.
  • Spam, advertising, repeated content, coordinated manipulation, automated submissions, or attempts to evade safeguards.
  • Instructions that encourage unsafe driving, trespass, interference with responders, illegal conduct, or obstruction of roads or railways.
  • Malware, links, code, or content unrelated to local travel awareness.

Reporting and enforcement

Use Report on a community update to flag a possible violation. Use Hide to remove it from this device immediately. Gridly may review, quarantine, remove, or decline to act on content; restrict an abusive source; preserve bounded audit evidence; and cooperate with lawful requests. Enforcement can occur without advance notice when needed for safety, privacy, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

A complaint does not automatically prove a violation. Reviewers examine content, context, freshness, complaint reason, duplication, and safety impact, then record a bounded outcome. Gridly does not publish reporter or source identifiers.

Your content and deletion

Use Delete mine on a report submitted from this device to create a verified deletion request. The device association is private and temporary. If it is no longer available, use the Privacy entry in Settings for assistance. Hiding content affects only this device; deletion or moderation affects the shared service.

You must be at least 18 to use Gridly. Gridly may restrict access for users Google has determined to be minors. Before the first community submission, and whenever this version changes, you must explicitly accept the Terms of Use and these Guidelines.
