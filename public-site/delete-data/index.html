Privacy request

Delete Gridly Data

Product: Gridly

Operator: DJ Burns Collective LLC, doing business as Gridly App

Gridly does not create consumer user accounts. Local profiles and preferences are not consumer accounts, and you do not need to create an account to request deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. Use “Delete mine” when the original device is available. Open the community report submitted from that device and choose Delete mine. Gridly uses the private, temporary device association to verify the request.
  2. Contact Gridly if same-device verification is unavailable. Email privacy@gridlygo.com. Gridly will apply the minimum verification necessary to review the request without disclosing another person's information.

What to include

Provide enough context to help locate the report, such as its approximate time and place, and explain that you are requesting deletion. If available, include other report context you already know. Public knowledge of a report's time or place alone is not proof of ownership, so Gridly may ask for additional information reasonably necessary to verify your authority and locate the record.

Do not initially send passwords, government identification, or unrelated sensitive documents. Gridly will not require you to create a consumer account.

What is deleted

When a verified request is completed with a deletion outcome, Gridly removes the public community report. Database cascades remove its private device link and live submission receipt. Completion also clears the deletion request's target-report reference and requester digest.

Gridly will tell the requester what categories were deleted, quarantined, denied for a documented lawful reason, or could not be located. An exceptional review may quarantine a report from public view while the review proceeds; normal bounded retention still applies.

What may be retained

  • One-way replay evidence without a report or device reference may remain so an old submission operation cannot be replayed.
  • A bounded completion record and the minimum case evidence required for legal, security, audit, and operational purposes may remain for the period described below.
  • Non-identifying monthly condition-family counts may remain. Gridly does not describe retained or de-linked information as anonymous unless the implementation demonstrates that it cannot reasonably be relinked.
  • Provider-controlled logs, correspondence, infrastructure records, and backups follow applicable provider practices, operational schedules, and legal obligations. Gridly does not promise deletion from a third-party provider's systems or a provider deletion date without verification.

Bounded retention periods

Community reports and private device linksScheduled for deletion by day 149 from original submission and never linked beyond 180 days.
Complaint and deletion-request linkagesCleared by day 149.
Completed deletion requestsShortened to a no-more-than-90-day completion window.
Source suppressionsExpire no later than the associated report's linkage deadline and never beyond 180 days.

Updating, confirming, clearing, exporting, restoring, moderating, or requesting deletion of a report does not restart its linkage period. Gridly-controlled restores must not reintroduce an expired report row or private link.

Local and provider data

Clearing Gridly app or site data can remove local acceptance, settings, hidden-report identifiers, and saved places. It does not recall server records, provider records, email, or copies independently retained by other users or providers. Report disappearance from the map is not by itself proof that every database, cache, export, backup, or provider copy has been deleted.

Questions or appeals

Email privacy@gridlygo.com. Identify the response if you are asking Gridly to review or appeal an earlier decision. Applicable legal rights, deadlines, and regulator complaint rights are not limited by this page.
