Texas is the focus

From rural communities to major cities.

Gridly is designed around the scale and variety of travel in Texas. Product coverage varies by place and source; the planned initial availability does not imply nationwide condition coverage.

Company

Developed by DJ Burns Collective LLC

DJ Burns Collective LLC develops Gridly as a Texas-focused travel-awareness product. Gridly is software designed to help people gather useful local context before they go.

The product is being prepared as a consumer mobile app for a coordinated Apple App Store and Google Play launch. The planned consumer launch is for adults 18 and over.

Use with official guidance

Awareness supports decisions. It does not replace authority.

Gridly keeps official-source information attributed where it appears. It does not replace emergency instructions, roadway authorities, weather agencies, railroad signals or navigation services. Conditions can change quickly; always follow official directions and what you can safely observe.

Mobile availability
