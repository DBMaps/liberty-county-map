# LP192 — San Antonio consumer-region consolidation design

**Status:** `CONSUMER_REGION_CONSOLIDATION_DESIGN_OWNER_APPROVED_WITH_PROVISIONAL_NAMING_AND_FAR_SOUTHWEST_HOLD`

**Recommendation:** `READY_TO_MERGE_LP192_DESIGN`

## Governance conclusion

Thirty SA Tomorrow atomic units are appropriate for City planning governance but too granular and administrative for normal consumer selection. The atomic layer remains preserved because it is the certified identity and geometry substrate; consumer labels are a separate, reversible product design layer. This report recommends nine regions, does not activate any region, and emits no runtime identity.

All 33 PLACE/CDPs remain independent under `INDEPENDENT_GOVERNED_PLACE_WINS`. Raw intersection is not membership: LP191's 2 TRACE and 39 MINOR relationships are context only, and even the 2 MATERIAL relationships cannot override independent governance.

## Source and Houston product-language evidence

The design uses LP191, the official registry, topology/working-geometry governance, West Northwest certification, and the Far Southwest hold. Houston evidence supports recognizable anchors (Downtown, Medical, airport/activity districts), explicit independent-community exclusions, and separating design from activation. Houston names and runtime definitions were neither treated as San Antonio authority nor changed.
