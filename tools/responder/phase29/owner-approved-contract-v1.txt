GRIDLY DISPATCH — APPROVED PHASE 29 DURABLE REPORT CONTRACT
==================================================

PURPOSE

Build the durable Dispatch reporting foundation correctly from the beginning for:

- Police
- Fire
- EMS
- Public Works

Operational onboarding order:

Police → Fire/EMS → Public Works

The onboarding sequence does NOT authorize architectural deferral. All four department classifications must be supported by the durable contract from the beginning.

==================================================
1. NORMALIZED REPORT MODEL
==================================================

A report consists of three independent dimensions:

1. report family/subtype — what happened
2. structured road/travel impact — how travel is affected
3. timing — PLANNED or UNPLANNED

A crash that closes a roadway remains one TRAFFIC_COLLISION report with structured FULL closure impact.

Do NOT require duplicate crash + closure records.

4. ROAD IMPACT IS NOT A DUPLICATE REPORT CATEGORY
==================================================

The following are impact values, not separate cause reports:

- lane restriction
- partial blockage
- full closure
- alternating traffic
- roadway narrowing

They remain first-class product/reporting choices but are stored as structured impact attached to the underlying cause report.

5. ROAD / TRAVEL IMPACT CONTRACT
==================================================

A report may contain multiple independently identified impact segments.

direction_affected:

NORTHBOUND
SOUTHBOUND
EASTBOUND
WESTBOUND
BOTH_DIRECTIONS
ALL_DIRECTIONS
NOT_APPLICABLE
UNKNOWN

lane_extent:

NONE
ONE
MULTIPLE
ALL
UNKNOWN

lanes_affected_count:
optional positive integer

total_lanes_count:
optional positive integer

closure_extent:

NONE
PARTIAL
FULL
UNKNOWN

traffic_operation:

NORMAL
ALTERNATING
NARROWED
STOPPED
UNKNOWN

public_passable:

YES
NO
UNKNOWN

emergency_vehicles_passable:

YES
NO
UNKNOWN

start_point:
optional validated coordinates plus governed road/segment reference

end_point:
optional validated coordinates plus governed road/segment reference

duration_state:

ESTIMATED
UNKNOWN

estimated_end_at:
required only when duration_state = ESTIMATED

reason_subtype:
normalized report subtype reference

attribution:
server-derived organization and reporting department/unit

last_confirmed_at:
trusted server-recorded confirmation timestamp

timing:

PLANNED
UNPLANNED

scheduled_start_at:
required for planned impacts

effective_start_at:
actual activation time

impact_status:

SCHEDULED
ACTIVE
CLEARED
EXPIRED
CANCELLED

valid_until:
mandatory bounded validity deadline

VALIDATION RULES

FULL closure requires:

lane_extent = ALL
traffic_operation = STOPPED
public_passable = NO

for the affected segment/direction.

Emergency vehicle access is independent.

“Passable with caution” requires:

closure_extent = PARTIAL
public_passable = YES

Narrowing / alternation can be specified separately.

If supplied:

ONE => lanes_affected_count = 1

MULTIPLE => lanes_affected_count >= 2

lanes_affected_count may not exceed total_lanes_count when total is known.

ALTERNATING traffic requires a partial restriction.

UNKNOWN must remain explicit and must never silently become YES/passable.

Confirmation cannot be future-dated.

PLANNED timing requires scheduled_start_at.

Known public geometry must remain within governed scope.

Private intake may accept incomplete information but incomplete intake must not automatically qualify for public publication.

Public projections must omit responder-only routing/access detail.

Operational workflow CLOSED must NOT be reinterpreted as road impact CLEARED.

Impact lifecycle and operational-record lifecycle remain independent.

RETAIN:

awareness.hazard.publish
awareness.road_closure.publish

These remain additional conjunctive HIGH-RISK gates.

They are NOT:

- inheritable parent permissions
- blanket publication permissions
- substitutes for subtype keys

HAZARD SUBTYPE

requires:

narrow subtype capability
AND awareness.hazard.publish

FULL ROAD CLOSURE

requires:

narrow subtype capability
AND awareness.road_closure.publish

HAZARD-CAUSED FULL CLOSURE

requires all three:

narrow subtype capability
AND awareness.hazard.publish
AND awareness.road_closure.publish

LANE RESTRICTION / PARTIAL BLOCKAGE

requires:

narrow subtype capability
AND permitted impact envelope

PLANNED closure still requires road-closure authority.

A capability grant binds:

- organization
- explicit eligible department/unit set
- exact subtype capability
- governed scope/version
- permitted impact envelope
- timing restriction
- review policy
- start/end
- renewal
- authority evidence
- issuing platform reviewer

Organization-wide grants MUST NOT silently authorize every department unit.

Existing broad condition/planned-work/official-notice keys remain historical compatibility identifiers and must not silently grant new subtype authority.

13. OFFICIAL PUBLIC NOTICE
==================================================

OFFICIAL_PUBLIC_NOTICE is a governed communication path.

It is NOT a safety or capability bypass.

Requires:

- notice capability
- bounded scope/audience
- documented communication authority
- current verification
- individual permission
- independent review

Required structured data:

- subject
- approved title/body
- issuing organization
- issuing department
- scope/version
- effective time
- mandatory expiry
- linked incident/impact when applicable
- revision/correction reference
- review decision

Prohibited:

- patient/clinical information
- investigative information
- tactical information
- victim/witness identity
- protected identity
- restricted infrastructure information

If a notice describes a hazard or closure, linked structured impact and corresponding hazard/closure gates remain mandatory.

Police activity communication uses this path.

Corrections:
new reviewed revision + invalidate previous active projection

Withdrawal:
immediate active-eligibility removal

Expiry:
cannot exceed earliest applicable grant, scope, verification, confirmation, or notice deadline

Audit:

- author token
- reviewer token
- source revision
- policy version
- decision
- correction/withdrawal reason

14. FIRE / EMS PROHIBITED DATA CONTRACT
==================================================

Dispatch is NOT a patient-care or clinical-record system.

Prohibited in Fire/EMS records, public OR internal:

- patient names
- patient identifiers
- diagnosis
- patient-linked symptoms
- treatment information
- patient-linked transport destination
- medical record numbers
- protected clinical narrative
- victim medical detail

ENFORCEMENT LAYERS

1. structured allowlisted intake
2. no clinical field definitions
3. bounded operational text
4. unexpected-key rejection
5. clear user warning
6. deterministic screening
7. content-classification screening as supplemental defense
8. quarantine/refusal before ordinary record/share/public flows
9. independent review
10. prohibited content cannot be approved by exception
11. minimized audit evidence
12. remediation path

Suggested states:

PENDING_REVIEW
APPROVED
REJECTED_PROHIBITED_CONTENT
QUARANTINED
REDACTED

Do not copy prohibited plaintext into ordinary logs/audit records.

Controlled redaction must preserve:

- event identity
- relationships
- redaction evidence

while removing prohibited plaintext under approved policy.

Automated screening MUST NOT be represented as a guarantee of legal/clinical compliance.
